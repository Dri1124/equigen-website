# EquiGen Health Foundation — Development Website

This repository is a **development workspace** for consolidating EquiGen's educational pages. It is not the production website.

## Pages being consolidated
- Home
- Genetics 101 (Cookbook / Blueprint paths, shared genetics lessons)
- Protein Kitchen
- Familial Hypercholesterolemia (FH), including LDL, HDL, triglycerides and total cholesterol
- Hereditary Breast Cancer
- History, Trust & Your Health
- Get Involved

## Design agreements
- Reuse the existing EquiGen logo and exact navigation order: Our Purpose; Why It Matters; Our Commitment; Learning Hub; Get Involved.
- Georgia headings; Arial body; purple/lavender/cream.
- Represent Black women and girls across the full range of skin tones, ages, hair textures and styles.
- Use realistic photography or photorealistic images for analogies and accurate scientific diagrams for biological processes.
- Mobile-friendly, accessible navigation and educational interactions.
