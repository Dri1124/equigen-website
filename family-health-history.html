My Family Health History

Knowing what conditions have affected your relatives can help you prepare informed questions for your healthcare team. You don't need every answer to get started.

Your privacy matters. This worksheet stays on your device unless you choose to print, save, or share it. Avoid entering sensitive details on shared devices. You can leave any question blank. You do not need to collect relatives' medical records or genetic test reports without their permission.

2. Conditions in my family

List biological relatives when known, on either side of your family. Include the condition and approximate age at diagnosis if you know it. An unknown history is okay.

Relative / side of familyHealth condition or relevant findingAge at diagnosis (approx.)

Examples to ask about: heart attack, stroke, very high LDL cholesterol, breast or ovarian cancer, other cancers, and any known inherited condition. Other diagnoses may also be relevant.

3. Important details I may want to ask about

Heart and cholesterol

Has anyone had very high LDL cholesterol, an early heart attack or stroke, or a diagnosis of familial hypercholesterolemia (FH)?

Cancer and genetic testing

What types of cancer occurred, and at what ages? Has anyone shared a genetic diagnosis or test result?

4. Questions for my healthcare professional

Check the questions that are relevant to you, or add your own.

5. My next step

Remember: Family history can provide useful clues, but it does not confirm or rule out a condition. Some people have limited access to biological family history. A healthcare professional can help interpret what is known.
