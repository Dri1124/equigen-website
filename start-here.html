Not sure which topic fits?

Begin with Genetics 101, then explore a health topic that interests you. You can return here at any time. EquiGen's goal is to help you ask informed questions—not to diagnose a condition.

Three steps you can take today

  1. Write down what you know. Record relevant health conditions and ages at diagnosis in your family.
  2. Notice patterns. Repeated diagnoses or unusually early disease may be worth discussing with a clinician.
  3. Bring questions to your appointment. Ask whether screening, further evaluation, or genetic counseling is appropriate for your situation.
